# Approved voice and density example

Style calibration only, not a price list or evidence of live project capabilities. Replace topic-specific content for the current brief. Do not automatically reuse the opening or closing.

## Example post

got a few ideas for claus. bit of a brain dump, but curious what you think.

1. offer token launches and token/dao management.
help projects launch, set up fees, manage treasuries and run votes. charge for setup and ongoing work. saves teams from having to handle all of it themselves.

2. sell custom research on other projects' contracts and hooks.
combine code analysis with onchain data and paid sources like defillama. explain how the project works, who controls it, where the money comes from and what could go wrong. useful to traders, researchers, agents and other projects. copying api data won't sell. customers can connect their own agents to it.

3. offer recurring monitoring and personal reports.
you choose the projects, wallets or events. claus checks on a schedule and explains meaningful changes. a subscription to save you from checking everything yourself.

4. help projects build their own revenue models.
help them find something customers will pay for, set up payments and improve it over time. take a small share of revenue actually collected, say 1–2%, for that ongoing help.

one buyback rule for all four: an agreed share of claus's earnings after costs buys $CLAUS. publish the numbers and transactions.

which of these would you actually use?

## Why this is the reference

- Talks to readers; does not narrate an internal plan.
- Keeps launch/management, structural research, recurring monitoring and revenue curation distinct.
- Says what customers get and why paying may make sense.
- One hypothetical small share, not a page of invented tariffs.
- Paid data adds to analysis; the product is not an API copy.
- No repeated "i'd", price promise, forced thread or editorial preface.
- Common mechanism once, without pretending a buyback split is agreed.
